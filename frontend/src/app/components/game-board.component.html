How to play

You have 6 guesses to find teammates that connect the two players. A player is linked to another player if they have played together on the same team and year.

Take this example: The starting player is Vladimir Guerrero Jr. and the target player is Mike Trout.

Since Aaron Judge did not play with Vladimir Guerrero Jr, it is an INVALID guess

Since Alejandro Kirk played with Vladimir Guerrero Jr, but not with Mike Trout, it is a VALID guess, but not a winning guess.

The starting player shifts from Vladimir Guerrero Jr. to Alejandro Kirk. Now, you must find a teammate of Alejandro Kirk who has also played with Mike Trout.

Since Yusei Kikuchi played with both Alejandro Kirk and Mike Trout, it is a WINNING guess.

🎉 Victory!
